17.5 CONFIGURABLE SCORING RULES (core requirement)
Settings > Hiring > Scoring rules (company default) + per-job override in the
job wizard ("Scoring" step).
- Factors (each can be switched on/off, weight slider 0-100, total shown and
  must equal 100%, "auto-balance" button):
  skills match (AI semantic), must-have skills, nice-to-have skills,
  years of experience (min / ideal / max with a curve: 0 below min, 100 at
  ideal, gently lower above max), relevant-domain experience, location /
  distance to office (km bands or same city / relocation ok), notice period
  (days bands), expected salary vs band, education level, certifications,
  job stability (average tenure), recent use of key skills, languages,
  any custom field (numeric or yes/no).
- Knock-out rules (hard filters, shown separately): e.g. "must have
  Automation Anywhere", "notice <= 60 days", "location in Hyderabad or
  willing to relocate". Knocked-out candidates are flagged, not deleted.
- Live preview: pick 3 existing candidates and see their score change as
  you move sliders; "Save" creates a new version; history + revert.
- On save: background job recalculates scores for open applications of
  that job/company, with progress, and records old/new score.
- Candidate profile "Why this score": table of each factor, its weight, the
  candidate's value, points earned, plus evidence lines from the resume.
- Presets to start from (editable): "Balanced", "Experience first",
  "Location first", "Skills first", "Budget first".
- Sample file: scoring rules export/import (json/xlsx) so a client's rules
  can be copied to another job or client.

17.6 AUTOMATED SHORTLISTING (like Jobs)
- Per job "Shortlisting rules" step in the job wizard: shortlist threshold,
  knock-outs, max shortlist size, auto-shortlist on/off, undo window
  (default 4 business hours), who gets notified.
- When on: new/updated scores above threshold move to Shortlisted
  automatically, recruiter notified, undo button visible until the window
  ends, then scheduling starts per the job's scheduling mode.
- Shortlisting page: queue "Suggested by AI" / "Shortlisted" / "Rejected",
  bulk approve/reject with reason list, side-by-side compare (up to 3).
- Never auto-reject; rejection emails still go only after a person decides.

- Auto-check vs the job's salary band as soon as data arrives: In band ->

- Suggest an offer figure (within band, based on the rules) and pre-fill the
  offer CTC breakup; recruiter can edit before sending.
- Salary page = queue of candidates with status chips (Waiting for candidate,

17.15 REMOVE CANDIDATES (owner request)
- On candidate profile, candidate list (single + bulk select) and job
  pipeline: a "Remove" action. Only roles with permission
  candidates.delete (HR Admin by default; configurable) see it.
- Step 1 "Remove" = move to Recycle bin (soft delete): hidden from all lists,
  searches, reports, scoring and emails; scheduled emails/interview offers
  for that candidate are cancelled; can be restored within N days
  (setting, default 30).
- Step 2 "Delete permanently" (from Recycle bin, or automatically when the
  N days pass): removes the candidate, applications, interviews,
  negotiation/confirmation tokens, notes, scores, AI results, uploaded
  files (resume, photo, BGV docs) from storage, and personal data in the
  message log (kept as "removed candidate" without name/email). Keep only
  an audit entry: who, when, why, counts - no personal data.
- Before deleting, show a clear summary: "This removes 1 candidate, 2
  applications, 1 interview, 3 files. This cannot be undone." + type the
  word DELETE to confirm. Reason is required (list: duplicate, test data,
  candidate request (privacy), withdrawn, other).
- Blocked (with a plain explanation) if the candidate has an accepted offer
  or is already an employee - use the exit process instead.
- Privacy request path: "Candidate asked to delete their data" goes straight
  to permanent delete after confirmation (DPDP erasure) and records it.
- Bulk remove up to 500 at a time as a background job with progress.
- Recycle bin page under Candidates with Restore / Delete permanently.
- Proof in FIXES_17.md: remove -> restore -> remove -> permanent delete on a
  Demo candidate, with the DB/storage counts before and after.
