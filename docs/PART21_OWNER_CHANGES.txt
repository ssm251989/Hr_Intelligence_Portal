21.1 CLOSED JOBS ARE CLEANED UP AFTER N DAYS (configurable)
- Settings > Hiring > Closed jobs: "Move closed jobs to the Recycle bin
  after [30] days" (number, 0 = never). Also "Keep candidates of closed
  jobs in the talent pool" (on by default).
- Daily background job: for each job closed more than N days ago:
  job + its applications, screening results, scores, shortlist records,
  interviews, negotiation links, salary forms, documents -> Recycle bin
  (soft delete, same rules as 17.15: hidden everywhere, restorable).
  Candidates who were HIRED / are employees are never touched.
  If "keep in talent pool" is on, the candidate profile + resume stay in
  the talent pool (unlinked from the job); only job-specific data moves.
- 3 days before: job card shows "Will move to Recycle bin on <date>" with
  [Keep this job] (pauses cleanup for that job).
- Recycle bin: new "Jobs" tab next to Candidates, with Restore (brings back
  the job and its data) and Delete permanently. Items in the bin are
  deleted permanently after the existing recycle-bin retention (setting).
- Audit entry per run: jobs moved, counts.

21.2 WHEN A PERSON EXITS -> THEIR HIRING DATA GOES TO THE RECYCLE BIN
- Trigger: exit status Completed (after last working day + clearance).
- Moves to Recycle bin: candidate record and applications, screening
  results / AI scores, shortlist records, interviews + scorecards,
  negotiation / form links, salary forms, candidate emails (HR inbox),
  pre-joining portal data.
- KEPT (not moved): the employee record, signed offer letter, joining
  documents, BGV result, relieving/experience letters, exit record and
  exit interview, audit log - needed for HR/legal records. Retention for
  these follows Settings > Data & privacy > Retention.
- Settings > Onboarding & employees > After exit: choose which hiring data
  categories move to the bin (checkboxes, defaults as above) and "after
  [0] days from exit completion".
- Candidate timeline note: "Hiring data moved to Recycle bin on exit
  (<date>)". Restorable from the bin like any other item.

21.4 BACKGROUND CHECK: DOWNLOAD ALL + ASSIGN TO A BGV VENDOR
- BGV page and candidate BGV section:
  [Download all] -> one ZIP of all the candidate's BGV documents
  (folder per document type, file names "<Candidate> - <Document>.pdf",
  plus a summary PDF/Excel of statuses). Bulk on the BGV list: select
  candidates -> Download all (background job for large sets). Audit-logged.
  [Assign BGV vendor] -> pick a vendor from Agencies (type BGV), choose
  checks (education, employment, address, criminal, identity, reference,
  drug - list from the vendor's offered checks), due date -> vendor gets
  an email with a secure, expiring link (setting, default 14 days) to view
  /download ONLY this candidate's documents and upload the report.
  Status: Assigned -> In progress (vendor opened) -> Report received ->
  HR decision (Clear / Discrepancy / Failed / Resend form - existing HR
  decision step). Reminders to the vendor before/after due date.
  Reassign to another vendor; cancel assignment (link stops working).
- Report uploaded by the vendor appears in the candidate's BGV section,
  HR notified. Vendor never sees other candidates.
