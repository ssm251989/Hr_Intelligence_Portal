HR Automation - installation

1. Unzip this file (right-click > Extract All). Keep the folder together.
2. Double-click HRAutomation.exe and click "Yes" when Windows asks for permission.
3. The setup screen opens in your browser. Fill in:
     - your company name
     - your name, work email and a password (you will sign in with these)
     - two database passwords (keep them somewhere safe)
   then click Install. Everything else is done for you: the database, the tables,
   your company, your admin account and the Windows services.
4. When it says "HR Automation is installed", click "Open HR Automation".
   A shortcut "HR Automation" is on your desktop.

Passwords: at least 10 characters, with a letter and a digit. Symbols such as @ # are
fine. Line breaks, tabs and spaces at the start or end are not allowed.

Needs: Windows 10/11 or Windows Server 2016+, 64-bit, 4 GB RAM, 2 GB free disk.
Nothing else has to be installed first.

Later: double-click HRAutomation.exe again (or the desktop shortcut) to open it.
Other computers in the office open http://<this-computer-name>:8080 (tick
"Let other computers on the office network open it" during setup).

Help: contact CTD Techs.
